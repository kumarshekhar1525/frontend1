2000 → 2026 India Fintech Evolution Timeline

Explore how India transformed from physical paper banking to world-leading AI Autonomous Finance.

2000

Paper Banking & Cheques

Manual branch queues, Demand Drafts, and paper cheque clearing taking 3-5 days.

2004

RTGS Launch

Real Time Gross Settlement introduced by RBI for high-value interbank transfers.

2005

NEFT Launched

National Electronic Funds Transfer enabled batch-based bank transfers across India.

2010

IMPS 24x7 Realtime

Immediate Payment Service created instant mobile bank transfers 24/7/365.

2012

RuPay Network

NPCI launched India's indigenous debit & credit card payment network.

2014

Jan Dhan Yojana

Massive financial inclusion drive bringing 500M+ zero-balance bank accounts.

2016

UPI Revolution

Unified Payments Interface transformed India into the world leader in instant QR mobile payments.

2020

UPI AutoPay & Mandates

Seamless recurring payment mandates for subscriptions, EMIs, and SIPs.

2026

AI Finance & FinHub
